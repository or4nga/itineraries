DAY 9Wednesday, December 30🌊

Zion — THE NARROWS

The single most unique, physically immersive experience of the entire 13-day trip. You wade UP a slot canyon river through walls that soar hundreds of feet overhead. Your boys’ love of Big Bend tells you they are absolutely built for this.

Before you go — reserve gear at Zion Outfitter: 📍 145 Zion Park Blvd, Springdale · ☎️ (435) 772-5090 · zionoutfitter.com — ~$40–50/person · drysuit + neoprene socks + boots + poles · reserve in advance for holiday week

TimeActivity
Early AMZion Outfitter — pick up drysuits/boots/poles; check river flow with rangers.
MorningWalk Riverside Walk to river entry; wade into the Virgin River and go as far as comfortable.
Early PMReturn, warm up, return gear; hot showers and cocoa.
EveningEarly hearty dinner at Spotted Dog Café ; rest.

Adventure total: 2.2 mi paved + Narrows distance (as far as you go)

🌊 What to expect: The Narrows is unlike any hike in the world. You’re not hiking beside a river — you’re hiking IN it, through a slot canyon. The walls twist and turn, sometimes blocking the sky entirely. In December with a drysuit, it’s cold, thrilling, and completely safe. The boys will never stop talking about this.
